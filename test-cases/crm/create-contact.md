## Update 2026-10-15 — Contact Name and Email rules

- **Contact Name and Email are both mandatory** (TC:1, TC:11, TC:12).
- **Contact Name may be a duplicate** (TC:13): two Contacts can share a name as long as their emails differ.
- **Email must be unique** (TC:14–TC:16): saving an email that another Contact already has is blocked on create and on edit, with no "save anyway" option. TC:9 was changed accordingly: the non-blocking duplicate warning now covers a matching **phone** only.
- Still to confirm with the team: the exact duplicate-email error text, and whether email uniqueness ignores letter case and spaces (TC:15).
- TC:11–TC:16 are new and not yet automated.

## Notes for whoever picks this up next

TC:10 (audit trail) is **marked `fail` in ClickUp** already — the audit/history view may not currently show this correctly, or may not exist yet as a checkable UI element. Confirm with the team before automating. TC:6 explicitly depends on TC:5 having run first (the "create this Customer now?" prompt only appears after saving with a not-yet-existing Customer name), so they are one test (TC:5-6).

**TC:5-6 pass on dev (re-checked 2026-10-23).** Typing a name that matches no Customer offers the option "The Customer {name} does not exist. Will proceed to Customer creation after this Contact is saved."; picking it and saving shows the toast "Contact {name} created" and the prompt "Contact {name} was saved successfully! Proceed to create customer {name}?" (Cancel / Create customer). "Create customer" opens `/crm/customers/new?name=...&contactId=...&contactName=...` with Customer Name pre-filled and the Contact pre-selected under Link Contacts. Until 2026-09-16 there was no prompt and the typed name was dropped. Not yet live-probed against the running app — no locators confirmed; likely route is `/crm/contacts/new` based on the parallel confirmed for Create Customer (`/crm/customers/new`).
