# Techpack — Left-nav pages (Recents, Brands, Brand Requests, Trash)

- **User Story:** there is no ClickUp story for these pages; this doc was written from the live uat screens. Proposed: "As a Techpack user, I want quick ways into the catalogue, so I don't have to filter the full list each time: my recent files, one brand's packs, incoming brand requests, and cancelled packs."
- **Test-case set:** not yet created in ClickUp.
- **Automated in:** not yet automated (0 of 22).
- **Page objects:** none yet. The Dashboard's are [`techpack.page.ts`](../../src/pages/techpack/techpack.page.ts) and [`techpack.locators.ts`](../../src/locators/techpack/techpack.locators.ts); the shared left nav would belong there.
- **Latest run:** none. Every row below was observed live on uat on 2026-10-23, as alice (Admin) and bob (Qc-lead), during the redesign exploration.

These pages arrived with the FloorOS redesign (2026-10-22). They hang off the Techpack left nav (`complementary "Techpacks"`). The Dashboard itself is in [`techpack-list.md`](techpack-list.md), and Validation rules is in [`validation-rules.md`](validation-rules.md).

**Test data:** these cases are read-only. Any techpack or customer they rely on is a QA-AUTO record created by the suite, carrying "QA-AUTO — … test data (pw-hybrid-framework). Safe to delete." in its Description/Remark. Never record a brand request, and never toggle an Auto-Generate switch, on a real brand.

## Notes

- **Pages and routes:** Recents is `/techpacks/recents`, Brand Requests is `/techpacks/brand-requests` (with settings at `/techpacks/brand-requests/settings`), Brands is `/techpacks/brands`, and Trash is `/techpacks/?scope=trash`.
- **Brand pages:** each brand has its own Dashboard at `/techpacks/?customer=<code>`.
- **Locators:** scope them to `getByRole('main').last()`; there are two nested `main` elements. "New request" needs `exact: true`, because the tab "New Request N" also matches.
- **Not covered yet:**
  - recording a brand request (it creates data, and what enables "Record" wasn't explored)
  - Recents/Brands "Split view"
  - Trash row actions (uat had no Cancelled techpacks on 2026-10-23)
